FBA Preparation Process

A structured preparation workflow for eligible seller inventory requiring FBA preparation and packaging support.

01. Receive

Inventory is received and organized for preparation.

02. Inspect

Products and quantities are checked according to the agreed preparation process.

03. Prepare

Applicable labeling, wrapping, bundling and other preparation activities are completed.

04. Package

Products are packaged according to the applicable requirements for the inventory.

05. Shipment Ready

Prepared inventory is organized for shipment to the designated Amazon fulfillment location.
